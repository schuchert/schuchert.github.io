## 🎯 The 3+2 Principles of Deep Flexibility

Before you stretch, remember these five rules to ensure your nervous system allows muscle fibers and fascia to lengthen:

* **Wet Noodle:** Relax your entire body. Do not tense or fight the stretch. Lock out your bones or use blocks/pillows so you can let gravity do the work.
* **Breathe to Relax:** Use **4:8 breathing** (inhale for 4 seconds, exhale for 8 seconds). This stimulates your vagus nerve and down-regulates your nervous system's stretch reflex.
* **Passive Tension Over Time:** Hold poses for **2 to 5 minutes**. It takes about 2 minutes for the nervous system to relax and allow the fascia to release.
* **Feels over Looks:** We target a **7/10 tension scale**. If you cannot speak comfortably, you are too deep. Back off, make adjustments, and use props.
* **Work from where you are:** Respect recent injuries or tightness. Keep moving, but work at a gentle 4-5/10 tension if needed.

## 📆 5-Day Hips Routine

If you want to focus specifically on balanced hip strength and deep flexibility, try this 5-day rotation:

* 📅 **Day 1:** Strength (Psoas Hold, Floor Mountain Climbers) + Stretches (Blaster, Pigeon)
* 📅 **Day 2:** Strength (Lunge, Straight Leg Raise) + Stretches (Jackknife Blaster, Butterfly)
* 📅 **Day 3:** Strength (Half Kneeling Flexor Stretch, Psoas Hold) + Stretches (Blaster, Half Happy Baby)
* 📅 **Day 4:** Strength (Floor Mountain Climbers, Leg Raise) + Stretches (Jackknife Blaster, Pigeon)
* 📅 **Day 5:** Strength (Lunge, Half Kneeling Stretch) + Stretches (Blaster, Half Happy Baby)
